# Inicio

## Scope and mode

Single-page institutional website. Visitor mode: Persuade, governed by trust and access rather than commercial urgency.

## Audience, job, and action

People and families in Colima facing a significant loss need to understand what support exists, recognize a credible local institution, and request help. The single primary action is `Solicitar apoyo` through the published WhatsApp number.

## Proof and content

Use the real organization name and logo, original mission and vision, three existing services, six published team profiles, real institutional photography, the Jornadas Tanatológicas 2023 archive, and the physical address. Do not invent claims, outcomes, schedules, pricing, testimonials, licences, or metrics.

## Direction

An institutional living archive: documentary photography, committed purple fields, precise gold rules, and an editorial hierarchy. It should feel humane, composed, local, and established without becoming clinical, funereal, or commercially urgent.

## Memorable moment

The first viewport opens the support promise line by line while uncovering an authentic group photograph as documentary material, then resolves into a quiet three-part service index. Supporting sections reveal once through content-specific masks; reduced-motion mode remains static.

## Constraints

Static HTML/CSS/JS, WCAG 2.2 AA, progressive enhancement, responsive from 320 px, reduced motion, optimized raster assets, no dead links or unverified channels.

## Unresolved before launch

Official production domain, exact address wording, professional credentials, photo consent, privacy and crisis-support disclosures.
